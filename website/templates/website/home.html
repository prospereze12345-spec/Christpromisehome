Welcome to Christ Promise Children's Home

A place to grow, belong and hope

Christ Promise Children's Home is a Christ-centred home established in 2014 with a heartfelt commitment to providing children with love, care, protection, guidance and a strong foundation in God's Word.

We believe that every child is a precious gift from God. Our home is more than a place of shelter. It is a place where children can experience family, belonging, encouragement and hope while developing academically, emotionally, physically and spiritually.

Who We Are

Love, faith and purpose at the heart of our home

  • Our Story
  • Our Mission
  • Our Vision

Established in 2014, Christ Promise Children's Home exists to give children a safe and loving environment in which they can grow with dignity.

We welcome children from different backgrounds and circumstances and seek to care for each child with patience, respect and unconditional love.

Our mission is to provide children with a safe, loving and nurturing home where they can grow with dignity, receive quality education, develop their God-given potential and build a hopeful future.

We are guided by Christian values of love, compassion, integrity and service.

We want every child who comes through our doors to grow into a confident, responsible and caring person who can contribute positively to society.

Our hope is that a child's circumstances will never determine the limits of their future.

Child Safeguarding

Children deserve to feel safe, valued and protected

The safety, dignity and wellbeing of every child at Christ Promise Children's Home are among our highest priorities.

We are committed to providing an environment where children are protected from abuse, neglect, exploitation, discrimination and harm. Adults working with or supporting children are expected to maintain appropriate boundaries and uphold responsible safeguarding practices.

Every child has the right to feel safe, heard, respected and protected.
