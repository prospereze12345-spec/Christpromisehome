Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Christ Promise Children's Home collects through this website, why we collect it, and how it is kept safe.

Who we are

Christ Promise Children's Home is based at 20 Crescent Road, London E18 1HB. For any question about this policy or your information, contact us through our Contact page.

Information we collect

We only collect what is needed to respond to you:

  • Name, email address, subject and message when you use our contact form.
  • Email address, phone number and message when you make a donation enquiry.
  • Details you choose to share when applying to volunteer.

We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website, and this website is not directed at children.

How we use your information

We use the information you give us only to reply to your enquiry, process a donation, or consider a volunteer application. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Photographs of children

Photographs of children in our care are only published with appropriate consent, and are chosen and captioned carefully so that a child cannot be identified or located by a stranger. If you have a concern about an image on this site, please contact us and we will review it promptly.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies necessary for it to function correctly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry and donation correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to deal with your request and meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, contact us through our Contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last reviewed.

Questions about your data or this policy? Get in touch via our Contact page.
