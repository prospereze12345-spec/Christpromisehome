Christ Promise Children's Home is a Christ-centred home established in 2014, committed to providing children with a safe, caring and supportive environment.

We believe every child deserves to be loved, protected, educated and given the opportunity to build a hopeful future. Our work focuses on creating a stable home where children can grow in confidence, character and purpose.

Our mission is to provide children with love, care, protection and opportunities for education and personal development.

Through a Christ-centred environment, we seek to support each child emotionally, academically, socially and spiritually while helping them develop the confidence and skills needed for a positive future.

Our vision is to see children grow into confident, responsible and purposeful young people who can contribute positively to their communities.

We aspire to create a home where every child has the opportunity to discover their potential and build a hopeful future.

What Guides Us

Our Values

Love & Care

We provide a caring environment where children feel valued, supported and respected.

Education

We believe education gives children the knowledge, confidence and opportunities they need to grow.

Faith & Purpose

We encourage children to develop strong character, faith and a meaningful sense of purpose.

Safeguarding

The safety, protection and wellbeing of every child remain central to everything we do.

Community

We value strong relationships and cooperation with people who share our commitment to children.

Hope

We work to give every child the confidence to look forward to a brighter and more purposeful future.

Why Trust Us

Committed to Transparency and Impact

Accountable Care

Every donation and resource is directed toward the direct wellbeing, education and safety of the children in our care.

Rigorous Safeguarding

All staff, volunteers and partners operate under strict child-protection standards designed around each child's dignity and safety.

Lasting Impact

Since 2014, our approach has focused on long-term outcomes — education, character and faith — rather than short-term relief.
